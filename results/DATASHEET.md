# Datasheet: the BioModels census behind "Every line is a loop"

Generated by `analysis/data_audit.py`. Every number below is read from `results/data_audit.json` (key in brackets) or from the results file named beside it. Rerun the script to refresh both.

In short:

- 640 release 31 models narrow to 392 with a behaviour label and 277 filed in the atlas; the 435-model hold-out narrows to 278 and 191.
- 32 hold-out models are tied to a release 31 model by derivation, publication or authorship.
- Counting one model per group changes no address-test verdict (10 of 10 recounts with a verdict FAIL, as published) and no sign-rule or gain verdict. The hold-out "follows" line fails by one model as published; it passes in 6 of 10 recounts (one per publication group; publication groups weighted equally; one per lineage group; lineage groups weighted equally; author-year groups weighted equally; lab groups weighted equally) [verdicts under grouping].

## 3. How it was collected

- Modellers chose what to publish. BioModels curators chose what to curate and checked each model against its paper.
- Release 31 is a snapshot of that library in 2017. The hold-out is what curators added afterwards, read in 2026.
- No model was edited. Every script runs on the files as downloaded.

## 12. Known biases

- Selection: modellers publish what worked and what was interesting, and curators curate a subset. Clocks, switches and famous systems are over-represented. The census cannot say how common a circuit is in cells.
- Behaviour is read at published rates only. What a model does at other rates is not seen.
- Some systems are drawn many times (section 7). Counts treat each model as one vote unless recounted by group (section 9).
- Names, years, systems and first authors are read from model names by rules in the script, so a few are wrong.
- Every per-model limit counts work (budget.py), so a slower or busier machine files the same models; a 6-hour wall-clock safety net only stops a runaway.

## 13. What is not done

- No second reader. One person decided the hold-out hand check of five sign-rule candidates (`holdout_handcheck.json`), the six truth-test families (`analysis/truth_tests.py` FAMILIES) and the scores of chapter 07's squares. The blind read of 20 "follows" traces is not done (`holdout_score.json` "six answers"."blind read").
- Exact duplicates are found only when two files declare their parts in the same order; re-encodings of one paper are caught through the shared publication instead.
- Publication years come from model names; the papers' own records were not fetched. Taxon names are from memory.
- Choices set or kept after results were seen: 10 (filters applied to braking loops; running total, paired; atlas: step, moved, exact, memory gap, 40 parameters; wall-clock safety net; work budget: dynamics; work budget: truth tests; work budget: atlas; work budget: robust return; families of one system (truth tests); address test protocol and its amendment).
